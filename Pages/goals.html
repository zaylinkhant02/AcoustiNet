United Nations SDG 3

Health is Quiet

Noise pollution is the second largest environmental cause of health problems, just after air quality. AcoustiNet spreads awareness about SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being.

53dB

WHO Health-Safe Limit

45dB

Sleep Protection Standard

SDG 3: Prevention & Wellbeing

AcoustiNet aligns with Target 3.4 of the UN Sustainable Development Goals, focusing on the reduction of environmental stressors that contribute to non-communicable diseases. Specifically, we aim for SDG 3.4.1.

Cardiovascular Protection

Reducing road traffic noise to meet the WHO guideline to mitigate long-term risks of hypertension.

Sleep Disturbance Mitigation

Targeting the WHO night-noise limit to prevent chronic sleep fragmentation and metabolic stress.

TARGET SDG 3.4.1

Designing for Better Living

Our project proposes a new way to look at our streets. Instead of just accepting noise as part of city life, we use the SDG 3 framework to show how calmer environments can improve daily well-being for everyone.

Raising Awareness

We want to help people understand that quiet isn't just a luxury; it's a necessary part of staying healthy and reducing stress.

Better Urban Habits

We suggest how small changes in traffic or landscape design can create peace in busy neighborhoods.

"The goal of this project is to prove that smart urban planning can be a tool for preventive health, making cities more liveable for the next generation."

A Scalable Framework

Our proposal is designed to be adaptable. While we focus on specific urban areas for our project, the methods we use to identify and protect soundscapes can be applied to any neighborhood looking to improve public health.

Community Ownership

The project encourages local residents to participate in identifying the quiet areas they value most, ensuring the design meets actual community needs.

Future Integration

Our sustainable goal is for this project to help serve as useful information for future city planners to integrate acoustic health into standard urban infrastructure projects.
