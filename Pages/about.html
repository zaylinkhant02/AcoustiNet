About Us

Dedicated to preserving urban calm

We are a team focused on finding the intersection between smart technology and human wellbeing. AcoustiNet is our answer to the growing problem of urban noise pollution.

Our Mission

To provide neighborhoods with the tools they need to track noise. We want to help people find quiet places easily and encourage everyone to be mindful of the sound they make in shared spaces.

Our Vision

A future where every city uses smart data to protect the health of its citizens. We imagine a world where quiet zones are a standard part of urban living, making cities more peaceful for everyone.

What We Value

Our Goals

Point 01

Keeping Things Private

We believe that everyone has the right to privacy. Our sensors are built to never record what people say. They only look at how loud the noise is, not who is making it and it doesn't collect any personally identifiable data or audio.

Point 02

Helping the Neighborhood

Our goal is to make our local area a better place to live. By finding out which parts of the city are too loud, we can help keep parks and libraries quiet and peaceful for everyone.

Point 03

Open for Everyone

We think data should be easy to see. We make sure our charts are simple and clear so that anyone in the community can check the noise levels whenever they want.

Our Focus

Who We Support

Students

Ensuring that library study zones remain under the 50dB threshold for deep focus and learning.

Residents

Monitoring local parks to preserve natural soundscapes away from traffic and construction noise.

Local Leaders

Providing actionable data that can be used to make better decisions about urban quiet zone management.

Target Areas

Quiet Zones

Educational Spaces

For silence in libraries that help students focus on their studies without noise distractions.

Public Sanctuaries

Such as local parks to ensure residents have a peaceful place to relax away from city traffic.

Quiet Work Zones

For quiet outdoor seating and workspace areas where people can work peacefully and relax.

Our Story

AcoustiNet began as a collaborative effort to address the often-overlooked issue of noise pollution in our immediate environments. We noticed that while air and water quality are frequently monitored, the acoustic health of our libraries and parks remained largely unquantified.
